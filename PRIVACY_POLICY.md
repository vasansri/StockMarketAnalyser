# Privacy Policy for StockVision

Last updated: February 2024

StockVision ("we," "our," or "us") provides a stock market analysis platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection
- **Personal Information**: We collect your email address and name if you sign up via Email or Google Auth.
- **Financial Data**: We do not store your actual brokerage passwords. We only track the stock holdings you manually add or sync.
- **Usage Data**: We collect diagnostic data through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve app stability.

## 2. Use of Information
- To provide real-time market data and AI recommendations.
- To process premium subscriptions via Google Play Billing.
- To send you price alerts via background notifications.

## 3. Data Security
- We use industry-standard encryption for all data transmissions.
- Your authentication is handled securely by Firebase.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Finnhub / Alpha Vantage**: To provide market data.
- **Firebase**: For authentication and analytics.
- **Google Play**: For subscription management.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@stockvision.com.
